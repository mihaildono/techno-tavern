Input: lines of `id|source|title` (Bulgarian news, last 24h).

Task: find the story reported by the MOST different sources. Titles are worded
differently for the same event — group by event, not by exact words.

Rules:
- Group titles about the same event. Pick the group with the most distinct sources.
- Tie: pick the group with more articles. Still tied: pick the one containing a
  "Свободна точка" article.
- No group has 2+ distinct sources: use the FIRST "Свободна точка" line.
- Write one clear Bulgarian headline (max 90 chars) summarising the group.
- `sources`: distinct source names in that group. `ids`: their line ids.

Output ONLY this JSON, nothing else:

{"title":"","sources":[],"ids":[],"summary":""}

`summary` = 2 sentences in Bulgarian, facts from the titles only. Do not invent
details, numbers or names that are not in the input.
